Spreekpraktijk

Practise speaking Dutch by having real conversations.

Pick a situation, like drinks with colleagues or a visit to the town hall, and talk with an AI partner. After each reply you get short feedback in English. Made for A2–B1.

How it works

  1. Pick a scenarioChoose from 30+ everyday and work situations, or tell a longer story about your weekend.
  2. Speak or typeThe character talks first. Answer out loud with the microphone, or type.
  3. Learn from feedbackSee corrections, useful phrases and an idea for what to say next.

    What each button does

    • Settings (cog). On the scenario list. Change your provider, model or API key.
    • Spreek je antwoord in. Starts recording. It keeps listening through pauses. Click again to stop. Your words appear in the text box.
    • Send (paper plane). Sends your message. Pressing Enter does the same.
    • Annuleren. Shown for 5 seconds after you speak. Stops the automatic send so you can edit the text first. Clicking in the text box also stops it.
    • Question meaning. Help button. Shows the English meaning of what the character said.
    • Example answer. Help button. Shows a Dutch reply you could give, including a question to ask back.
    • Short. Help button. Makes replies shorter and cheaper.
    • Speaker (top right). Turns spoken replies on or off.
    • List icon. Shows or hides the summary of your corrections and good phrases. The summary never appears on its own.
    • Download icon. Saves the whole conversation as a text file. The summary panel has its own Download button for a separate summary file.
    • Scenario's. Goes back to the scenario list. This clears the current chat.
    • Small speaker next to a reply. Plays that line again.
    • Opnieuw genereren. Under the latest reply. Asks for a new reply and puts your message back in the box so you can change it.
    • Opnieuw. Appears after an error. Tries sending your last message again.
    • X. Closes the summary panel.

    What you need

    A browser (Chrome or Edge works best) and your own API key from Google Gemini, OpenRouter or Anthropic. A short conversation costs a fraction of a cent. You'll be asked for the key when you start. It stays in your browser and is never stored or used by this website.
